Indian School Muladha

In Pursuit of Excellence

An English-medium, co-educational CBSE school in Al Muladha, Oman, from Kindergarten through Grade 12.

From a beginning with 9 teachers and 90 students, ISML has grown into one of the largest schools outside Muscat’s capital area.

Nearly 2200 students and 98 staff, spread across 16 acres of lush green land.
